Delete your ClassDue account

This page explains how to delete your ClassDue account and the data stored for it. It applies to the ClassDue app by Usman Arshad.

Option 1: in the app (fastest)

  1. Open ClassDue and go to Settings.
  2. Scroll to Privacy & account and tap Delete my account.
  3. Confirm twice. Your account is deleted immediately.

Option 2: by email (no app needed)

Email usmanarshadjutt302@gmail.com from the email address you signed in with, with the subject Delete my ClassDue account. We will confirm and delete the account as soon as possible, and within 30 days at the latest.

What is deleted

  • Your account and sign-in record (email, name and picture received from Google).
  • Your Pro or trial record.
  • Any cloud backup of your planner.
  • Your email preferences and usage counters.

What is not deleted, and why

  • Homework on your phone. It is stored on your device, not on our servers. Remove it by deleting the app or its data.
  • Payment records. If you subscribed through Google Play, Google keeps its own billing records, and we may be required to keep accounting records. Cancel any subscription in Google Play too, because deleting your account does not cancel it.
  • Technical logs and provider backups are removed on their normal short schedule.
Deletion is permanent. We cannot recover an account or backup after it is deleted.
