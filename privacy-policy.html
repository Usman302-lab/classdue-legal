ClassDue Privacy Policy

Effective 10 November 2026 · Operated by Usman Arshad · Questions: usmanarshadjutt302@gmail.com

The short version. Your homework stays on your phone. We only receive it if you choose to back it up. We have no ads and no analytics or tracking. You can delete your account and its data at any time, inside the app.

We do not collect your location, contacts, advertising ID, or browsing activity. ClassDue contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools.

2. What stays on your phone

Your homework, timetable, grades, settings and reminders are stored on your device and work offline. Reminders are scheduled on your phone and are not sent through our servers.

3. App permissions

  • Notifications: to remind you about homework and classes.
  • Camera or photos: only if you choose to scan a timetable or attach a photo. The photo is used for that purpose only.
  • Internet: to sign in, use Pro features and, if you choose, back up.
  • Alarms and vibration: so reminders arrive on time.

4. Who we share data with

We do not sell your data and we do not share it for advertising. We use these service providers to run ClassDue, and they process data only on our behalf:

  • Supabase: hosting, our database, and sign-in.
  • Google: "Continue with Google" sign-in, and (when subscriptions launch) payment through Google Play.
  • An AI provider (currently DeepSeek): receives only the content of the AI requests described above, to generate a reply. Please don't put sensitive personal information into AI requests.

We may disclose information if the law requires it.

5. Where data is processed

Our providers may process data in countries other than your own. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards for those transfers.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your account and any backup until you delete them. When you delete your account, we delete your account, Pro record, backup, preferences and usage counters. Backups held by our hosting provider and technical logs are removed on their normal schedule. Payment records may be kept by Google Play and by law for accounting purposes.

7. Your rights and choices

  • Delete your account and its data: Settings → Privacy & account → Delete my account, or see our deletion page.
  • Export your homework at any time from Settings → Your data (files you can keep).
  • Opt out of emails at any time in Settings, or with the link in any email.
  • Ask us to access or correct your information, or to object to a use of it: usmanarshadjutt302@gmail.com. Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under laws such as the GDPR or UK GDPR, including the right to complain to your data-protection authority.

We use your email for promotions only if you opted in. We use it to run your account regardless.

8. Children

ClassDue is for students aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has given us data, email us and we will delete it. Where your country sets a higher age for consent, you need a parent or guardian's permission.

9. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections. Backups are private to your account and protected by database access rules so that only you can read them. No system is perfectly secure, so keep your Google account secure too.

10. Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will tell you in the app before it takes effect. The effective date is at the top.
